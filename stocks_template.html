How to read this page

The evidence. On the Nasdaq-100 as it was in January 2016 (no hindsight), the top 10% by momentum, held three months at a time and only while the S&P 500 was above its 200-day average, beat the average member by about 2.8% a quarter after costs (2016-2026, positive in both halves). It passed our strict test only just, the edge was much stronger after 2022, and it came from a few big winners rather than steady small gains.

What to expect. Some quarters the list will badly trail QQQ. Momentum can reverse sharply after market falls; the filter helps but will not catch every turn. Earnings dates are the biggest single-stock risk during a three-month hold.
