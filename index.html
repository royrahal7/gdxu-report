How to read this site

TESTED passed a test fixed in advance on years of data. EXPERIMENT being scored live, not yet trusted. CONTEXT useful background, not a forecast.

What the research found: GDXU's and gold's direction could not be predicted from public data at any horizon from 5 minutes to 2 weeks (price patterns, levels, macro drivers, seasonality, Fed days, positioning). What works is the size of moves (volatility, next-hour risk, the pre-open gap), the translation from gold to GDXU, gold's slow 12-month trend as a backdrop, and the fact that gold options usually price more volatility than follows.

Each range is wrong about one time in five. Rare events such as an early redemption of the ETN sit outside any model. Nothing here is investment advice.
