What this can and cannot tell you

It forecasts how far GDXU is likely to move, today and this week, not which way. Three direction models and eleven gold price-level signals were tested on up to twenty years of data, and none beat chance.

Each range will be wrong about one time in five, and rare events such as an early redemption of the note sit outside any model.
